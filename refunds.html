Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

At EducaPrática, we aim to offer a clear buying experience and digital resources that match the descriptions on our website.

1. Digital products

Our products are supplied in digital form, as PDF files available to download from the members’ area. Nothing is posted unless clearly stated.

2. 14-day money-back guarantee

Every purchase comes with a 14-day money-back guarantee, starting from the date of purchase.

Within these 14 days you can ask for a full refund for any reason, even if you have already downloaded the files. There is no fee for a refund.

This guarantee is in addition to your statutory rights. Under UK consumer law, the 14-day right to cancel online purchases can be lost for digital content that is supplied immediately with your express consent; our guarantee gives you a refund in this period regardless.

3. Faulty products

If a file will not open, is corrupted, does not match its description or has any other problem covered by consumer protection law, please contact us.

Depending on the case, you may be entitled to a repair, a replacement, a price reduction or a refund. These rights are not removed by downloading the product.

4. How to ask for help or a refund

You can ask for a refund directly through Cakto, the platform where you bought the product, or through the support channel shown in your order confirmation email and in the members’ area. Please include:

  • your order number or receipt;
  • the email address used for the purchase;
  • the reason for your request or a description of the problem (optional for guarantee refunds);
  • a screenshot, if it helps to show a fault.

Never send full card details or passwords.

5. Processing time

Once approved, refunds are processed by Cakto to the original payment method within 14 days. The time it takes for the money to appear in your account depends on your bank or card provider. Because the payment was made in Brazilian reais (BRL), the amount refunded in pounds may differ slightly because of exchange rate changes.

After a refund, access to the members’ area ends.

This policy does not affect your statutory rights.
