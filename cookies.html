Cookie Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small files or identifiers stored on your device. Cookies and similar technologies can help recognise sessions, remember preferences, understand how a website is used and measure advertising.

2. How we use cookies

Depending on the tools enabled, we may use:

Strictly necessary cookies: help the website work, keep it secure or provide features you ask for.

Preference cookies: remember choices you make on the website.

Analytics cookies: help us understand how visitors use the website and find improvements.

Advertising cookies: may be used to measure campaigns, understand interactions with adverts and show relevant advertising (for example, the Meta Pixel).

The categories actually used depend on the website’s current set-up and the providers integrated.

3. Consent and managing cookies

Non-essential cookies and similar technologies are only switched on after you give consent, as required by UK law (the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations).

You can accept, reject or choose categories of non-essential cookies through the cookie notice on the website, when it is shown. You can also block or delete cookies at any time in your browser settings.

Turning off some cookies may affect certain features, but non-essential cookies are never treated as required just to browse the website.

4. Third-party services

The website may use third-party services, such as analytics and advertising tools, when enabled. Checkout takes place on the Cakto platform, which uses its own cookies under its own policy. These services handle information under their own policies and settings.

More details about how we handle personal data are in our Privacy Policy.

5. Updates

We may update this policy to reflect changes in the technologies we use or in legal requirements.
