Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how EducaPrática collects, uses, stores and shares personal data when you visit our website or buy our digital resources.

1. Who is responsible for your data

The controller of your personal data is the person responsible for EducaPrática.

Website: materialpedagogico.vercel.app

2. What data we may collect

Depending on how you use the website, we may collect:

  • Identity and contact details, such as your name and email address;
  • Order information, such as the product bought, price, date and payment status;
  • Information needed to provide customer support;
  • Technical data, such as IP address, browser type, device and pages visited;
  • Information about where your visit came from (for example, UTM parameters) and how you interact with adverts, where these features are enabled and can lawfully be used;
  • Communication preferences and records of consent, where applicable.

Card details and other payment data are handled directly by Cakto, our payment provider. We do not store full card details.

3. How we use your data

We may use personal data to:

  • Process purchases and give you access to the products you bought;
  • Send order confirmations and information you need to access your products;
  • Respond to support requests;
  • Prevent fraud and keep the website secure;
  • Meet legal, accounting and tax obligations;
  • Understand how the website is used and improve our products;
  • Measure advertising results and carry out marketing, where we have a valid legal basis and any consent required.

4. Legal bases

Under UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018), we process personal data on one or more of these bases: performance of a contract, compliance with a legal obligation, legitimate interests that we have assessed, or consent.

Where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it at any time. This does not affect processing carried out lawfully before you withdrew it.

5. Sharing your data

We share only the data that is strictly necessary with service providers that support our operations:

  • Cakto: checkout, payment processing, members’ area and file delivery;
  • Vercel: website hosting and infrastructure;
  • Google Fonts: loading the website’s fonts (receives your IP address);
  • Analytics and advertising tools (such as Meta), where enabled;
  • Professional advisers or authorities, where needed to meet legal obligations.

We do not sell personal data.

6. Cookies and similar technologies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies for essential functions, preferences, analytics and advertising.

Non-essential technologies, such as certain advertising and tracking cookies, are only used in line with the applicable consent rules. See our Cookie Policy.

7. International transfers

Some of our providers process data outside the UK, including in Brazil (Cakto) and the United States. Where this happens, we rely on the transfer mechanisms and safeguards required by UK law, where needed.

8. Retention and security

We keep data only for as long as needed for the purposes described, including to meet legal and tax obligations and to resolve disputes.

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect data against unauthorised access, loss, alteration or disclosure. No system can guarantee complete security.

9. Your rights

Subject to legal conditions and exceptions, you may have the right to:

  • Access your personal data;
  • Correct inaccurate information;
  • Ask for your data to be deleted;
  • Ask us to restrict certain processing;
  • Object to certain processing;
  • Ask for your data to be transferred (data portability), where applicable;
  • Withdraw consent you have given;
  • Complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

To exercise your rights, contact us through the support channel shown in your order confirmation email or in the Cakto members’ area.

10. Marketing messages

You may receive marketing messages where we have a valid legal basis. Where consent is required, we will ask for it separately, and you can unsubscribe using the link in the message or by contacting us.

Messages needed to confirm purchases, give you access to products or provide support are not marketing messages.

11. Children’s privacy

The website is aimed at adults: parents, carers, teachers and educators. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

If you think a child has given us personal data, please contact us so we can look into it.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to the website, the services we use or legal requirements. The current version shows the date it was last updated.
